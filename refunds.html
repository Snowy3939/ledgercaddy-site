Refunds

Try it first. Refund within 30 days.

Last updated September 11, 2026

Try before you buy, and if it still isn't right, ask for your money back.

The short version

  • The 14-day free trial exists so you never have to buy blind.
  • If you do buy and LedgerCaddy isn't right for you, email us within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
  • No forms, no interrogation. One email.

30-day refund

If you are not happy with LedgerCaddy for any reason, email support@ledgercaddy.com within 30 days of your purchase with the email address you bought with. We will refund the full purchase price to your original payment method. Refunds are issued by our payment provider, the merchant of record, and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.

Once a refund is issued your license key is deactivated. Your books remain on your Mac and can still be opened, browsed, backed up and exported, so you are never locked away from your own records.

Maintenance renewals

Annual maintenance renewals can be refunded within 30 days of the renewal date, provided you have not yet installed a release that the renewal unlocked. A refunded renewal returns your updates window to what it was before.

After 30 days

After 30 days, purchases are final. If something is wrong with the app, please tell us anyway; we would much rather fix a problem than lose a customer, and we treat genuine defects generously.
