The download is the full app. It runs free for 14 days with every feature, no credit card. If you have bought a licence, enter your key in Settings and the trial notice goes away.

Download LedgerCaddy

Disk image (.dmg), about 45 MB. Signed and notarized by Apple for DART Management Inc.

Install in three steps

  1. Open the downloaded LedgerCaddy.dmg.
  2. Drag LedgerCaddy onto the Applications folder shown beside it.
  3. Open LedgerCaddy from Applications. The first time, macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet. Click Open.
