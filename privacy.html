Privacy

Your books stay on your Mac.

Last updated September 15, 2026

LedgerCaddy is built so that your books never leave your Mac. This page explains the little we do collect, and why.

The short version

  • Your books, transactions, attachments and backups stay on your Mac. We never see them.
  • The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.
  • The app never talks to the internet. Licence keys are checked on your Mac.
  • Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider and the merchant of record.
  • Product news arrives only if you opt in at checkout, and every message has an unsubscribe link.

The LedgerCaddy app

Your financial data. Every book you create is a single file stored where you choose on your Mac. Transactions, accounts, payees, categories, attachments, reports and automatic backups are all written to your Mac and nowhere else. LedgerCaddy does not upload, sync or transmit your books, and we have no way to access them.

No accounts, no tracking. LedgerCaddy does not require an account and does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any third-party tracking code.

Free trial. To run the 14-day trial the app stores a small record of the date you first opened it on your Mac. That record never leaves your Mac and contains no personal information.

Licence activation. When you enter a licence key, LedgerCaddy checks it on your Mac using a built-in public key. Nothing is sent anywhere: not the key, not your Mac's name, and never your books. There is no online check, then or later, so the app works the same with or without an internet connection.

Updates. LedgerCaddy does not check for updates automatically. New versions are downloaded from this website when you choose to.

Purchases

Purchases of LedgerCaddy are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and handles payment, taxes, receipts, licence keys and refunds. When you buy, you provide your email address and payment details to them, not to us. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy governs that information. We receive your email address and order details so we can deliver your licence and provide support.

Product news and offers

If you tick the box at checkout, LedgerCaddy will send you occasional email about updates to the app, related products from the same company, and offers. These messages come from LedgerCaddy and never from anyone else. Every message includes an unsubscribe link, and one click ends them. We do not sell, rent or share your address, and we never send this email to anyone who did not opt in. Leaving the box unticked changes nothing about your purchase, your licence or your support.

This website

This site is a static page with no cookies, forms or analytics of our own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record standard server logs such as IP address and browser type. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may also log the request. No other third-party services are used.

Support email

If you write to us, we keep your message so we can reply and follow up. We do not add you to any mailing list and do not share your address with anyone.

Children

LedgerCaddy is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top. Because we collect so little, changes will be rare.
