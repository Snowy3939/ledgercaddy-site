The approach

Just enough structure. Not too much.

Three things make books make sense: accounts (where the money sits), categories (what it was for) and payees (who you paid, or who paid you). LedgerCaddy gives you those, then gets out of the way. Record what happened, tick it off against your bank statement, and the reports are simply there.

No accounting degree required. A built-in guide walks every screen, and the Sample Book lets you try everything before you touch your own numbers.

About

About LedgerCaddy

LedgerCaddy was developed to provide a straightforward approach to bookkeeping: keep accurate, well-organized records and produce professional reports without the complexity of traditional accounting software.

It is designed around the needs of small businesses and individuals who want capable bookkeeping tools without unnecessary features. LedgerCaddy keeps each set of books in a single file on the user’s Mac, using the standard SQLite format, so financial records remain local, accessible, and under the user’s control.

LedgerCaddy reflects a simple philosophy: great records, professional reports, and bookkeeping that is easy to use.

Questions

Things people ask.

Is it really not a subscription?

Really. $50 USD buys LedgerCaddy outright with 12 months of updates. After that, $20 USD a year is optional. Skip it and the version you have keeps working forever.

Where is my data?

In one file on your Mac, wherever you choose to keep it. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. Back it up like any other document; LedgerCaddy also keeps automatic backups.

Can I bring in my existing records?

Yes. Import a CSV export from your spreadsheet or bank, and LedgerCaddy walks you through matching accounts and categories.

Will my accountant be happy?

That is the point. The Period Pack assembles the year-end reports accountants ask for into one PDF, and every report also exports to CSV.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. It is a native Mac app, not a web page in a window.
